Word list sources
=================

answers.txt
  Common five-letter words from SCOWL (Spell Checker Oriented Word Lists),
  size levels 10, 20 and 35 of the English and American lists, version
  2020.12.07. Inflected forms (plurals, past tenses) and offensive or obscure
  words were removed by hand. Every answer is also in allowed-guesses.txt.

allowed-guesses.txt
  Every five-letter word in the ENABLE word list (public domain), combined
  with SCOWL size levels up to 70 and the answers.
